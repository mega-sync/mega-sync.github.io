The free plan

Mega pricing begins at zero. Mega's own app listing describes the free plan as 20 GB of encrypted cloud storage with no credit card required, and says you can upgrade at any time for more space.

The same account signs in to the desktop app, so you can install the client and set up a sync before deciding on a paid plan. The download page lists the current version and the install steps.

Monthly or yearly

On the listed in app prices, a year of Pro I costs $124.99 against $149.88 for twelve monthly payments, a difference of $24.89. Pro II is $249.99 against $299.88, and Pro III is $374.99 against $449.88. In each case the yearly price is close to ten monthly payments.

Pro Lite is the exception: the listed yearly price of $79.99 is higher than twelve monthly payments of $6.49, which come to $77.88. That suggests the in app price list is not fully up to date, so treat every figure in the table as a guide and confirm the amount on Mega's site at checkout.

Renewal and cancelling

The listing states that subscriptions renew automatically for periods of the same length and at the same price as the first period. For purchases made through Apple, automatic renewal has to be switched off in the Apple account settings at least 24 hours before the next payment is due.

Those rules are for App Store purchases. If you buy on Mega's website, the renewal and cancellation steps are the ones in your Mega account and in Mega's terms.

Refunds and discounts

The listing does not state refund terms, and Mega's terms page was not read for this handbook. Do not assume a money back period; check the terms on Mega's site before paying.

No coupon codes appear in any source read for this page. The documented way to pay less is yearly billing on Pro I, Pro II or Pro III.

Which plan to look at

Start on the free plan and measure what you need to store. If the total fits in 20 GB, there is nothing to buy. If you need terabytes, Pro I is the one tier with a published size in the listing: 3 TB of storage and 3 TB of transfer.

The review covers how the sync and backup functions behave, and the alternatives page compares free tiers and encryption models of six other services. If you are on an older computer, check the system requirements before paying for storage you plan to sync.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mega free?

Yes. Mega's listing describes a free plan with 20 GB of encrypted cloud storage and no credit card required. You can upgrade later for more space.

How much does Mega Pro I cost?

Mega's US App Store listing shows Pro I at $12.49 a month or $124.99 a year as Apple in app prices, with 3 TB of storage and 3 TB of transfer. The price on Mega's website can differ and was not read for this page.

Does a Mega subscription renew automatically?

Yes. The listing states that subscriptions renew for periods of the same length at the same price. For App Store purchases, turn off renewal in your Apple account settings at least 24 hours before the next payment.

Can I get a refund from Mega?

Refund terms are not stated in Mega's app listing, and Mega's terms page was not read for this handbook. Check the terms on Mega's site before you pay.

Is there a Mega coupon or discount code?

None appears in the sources read for this page. Yearly billing on Pro I, Pro II and Pro III is listed at about the price of ten monthly payments.

What is the transfer quota on Mega?

The listing gives Pro I a transfer figure of 3 TB next to its 3 TB of storage. Transfer figures for the other plans are not stated in the listing.

See current prices on Mega's site

Web prices and plan sizes are set on Mega's own pricing page. Start free with 20 GB and upgrade there if you need more.
